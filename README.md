## Backlink Quality Checklist

Before evaluating a potential placement, consider:

1. Is the website relevant to the target audience?
2. Is the content useful, original, and editorially appropriate?
3. Does the website appear to serve a genuine audience?
4. Is the placement transparent and suitable for the campaign?
5. Are links handled according to applicable search engine policies?

## SEO Campaign Planning Checklist

- Define the business goal and target audience.
- Review existing content and technical SEO issues.
- Prioritize relevant, useful content improvements.
- Evaluate potential link opportunities for relevance and quality.
- Track work completed, qualified leads, and meaningful outcomes.
